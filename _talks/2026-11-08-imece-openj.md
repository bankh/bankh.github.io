Presentation of OpenJ at ASME IMECE 2026 (Paper No. IMECE2026-192465). OpenJ is a conceptual framework for an open-source digital human modeling and ergonomic assessment tool that integrates an anthropometric mannequin, posture prediction, standard ergonomic assessments (RULA, REBA, NIOSH, OWAS, Snook), and CAD integration via a Python core library and FreeCAD workbench.
