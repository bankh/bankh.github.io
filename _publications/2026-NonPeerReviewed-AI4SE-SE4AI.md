## 📌 Overview

**AI4SE and SE4AI Exploration: A Decade Looking Back and Forward** traces progress at the
intersection of artificial intelligence and systems engineering (SE) across three phases—a
**foundational** phase, an **applied** phase, and the current **large language model (LLM)
inflection** phase.

---

## Approach

The work performs a **human–AI agreement literature review**, assessing **1,712 INCOSE INSIGHT
articles** and **889 SERC publications** with **six AI models** to characterize how the field has
evolved and where it is heading. Accompanying **data and a web application** are shared to enable
comparison and reproducibility.

---

## Contributions

* A decade-long retrospective spanning the foundational, applied, and LLM-inflection phases of AI in SE.
* A large-scale human–AI agreement review across the INCOSE INSIGHT and SERC corpora using six AI models.
* Identification of **five critical research gaps** in AI4SE/SE4AI.
* Guidance for practitioners navigating **AI adoption, assurance, and workforce transformation** in systems engineering.
